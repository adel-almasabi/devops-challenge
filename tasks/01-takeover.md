# 01 — Take over the platform

If you are short on time, a **short prioritized list** here is enough. Do not spend the whole five
hours cataloguing every issue.

You're now the owner of everything in this repository. Before anything else breaks, you want to
know what you're dealing with.

## Context

- Budi is gone and unreachable. Assume nothing he wrote is necessarily true.
- Some things in AWS may have been created by hand. You can only see what's in this repo.
- On your first day, the security team mentioned: *"We have GuardDuty wired to Slack, but I'm pretty
  sure some findings never show up in the channel."*

## What we want

1. **A prioritized view of the risks** in what you've inherited. For each item: what it is, why it
   matters for *this* business, how urgent it is, and roughly how much work it would take to fix.
   We care more about your prioritization and reasoning than about how many items you find.
2. **Fix what you think must be fixed now**, in code. Explain why you picked those items, and how you'd
   roll the changes out safely given that some of them touch production.
3. **A short plan for your first 90 days** as the only engineer responsible for this platform.

Format and location are up to you.
